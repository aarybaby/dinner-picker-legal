[PARSLIA] helps you decide what to cook for dinner and lets you share food ideas with other users. This policy explains what we collect, why, and the choices you have.

## What we collect

**Account information.** If you create an account to use the community forum, we collect your email address, a password (stored securely by our authentication provider, never in readable form), and the display name you choose.

**Content you post.** Posts, comments, likes, and any reports or blocks you make. Posts and comments, along with your display name, are visible to other users of the app.

**Food preferences.** Your allergies, dietary restrictions, favorite cuisines, ingredients you dislike, and cooking effort are saved on your device. They are not tied to your account. When you search for meals, the relevant choices are sent as search filters so results fit your needs (see "Who we share data with").

**Technical data.** Our hosting providers may automatically process basic technical information such as IP address and request logs to run and secure the service.

We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, or advertising identifiers, and the app does not show ads.

## How we use it

- To provide meal suggestions that match your preferences
- To run your account and the community forum
- To keep the community safe, including reviewing reports and removing content that breaks our rules
- To keep the service secure and working

## Who we share data with

We use these service providers to run the app:

- **Supabase**: hosts our database, user accounts, and server functions. Your account and forum content are stored there.
- **Spoonacular**: provides online recipe data. Our server sends search requests that can include filters such as diet, allergies, cuisines, and ingredients to avoid. These requests are not linked to your email or display name.

We do not sell your personal information. We may disclose information if required by law or to protect the safety of users.

## Your choices and rights

- **Edit or clear preferences:** change them any time under Preferences in the app, or clear the app's data.
- **Delete your account:** in the app, go to Home → Account → Delete my account. This permanently removes your account, posts, comments, and likes. You can also request deletion at [your site]/delete-account.
- **Block and report:** you can block users and report posts or comments.
- Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete your data. Contact us at [your support email].

## How long we keep data

Account and forum data is kept until you delete your account. When you delete it, your data is removed from our database. Backups and logs may keep copies for a limited time before they are overwritten. [Adjust if you know your providers' retention periods.]

## Security

Data is transmitted over encrypted connections, and access to the database is restricted with access rules. No system is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

## Community content

Posts are public to other users. Please do not share personal information in posts. Content that is abusive, unsafe, or spam may be removed, and accounts may be suspended.

We may update this policy. The effective date above shows the latest version. Significant changes will be noted in the app.
