# Delete Some of Your Data

**App:** PARSLIA
**Developer:** A'arifah Alabi

You can ask for some of your data to be deleted without deleting your account.

## In the app

- **Posts and comments:** open the post, tap the ⋯ menu, and choose Delete post or Delete comment.
- **Food preferences:** change or clear them under Home → Preferences, or clear the app's data in your phone's settings.

## By email

For anything else, such as removing likes, blocks, or reports, email support@parslia-app.com from the address on your account with the subject "Delete some of my data" and tell us what to remove. We will reply within 30 days.

## Want to delete your whole account instead?

See [Delete your account](delete-account).
