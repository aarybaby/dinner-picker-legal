# Delete Your Account

**App:**[PARSLIA]
**Developer:** [A'arifah Alabi]

## In the app (fastest)

1. Open the app and sign in.
2. Go to **Home → Account**.
3. Tap **Delete my account** and confirm.

Your account is deleted immediately.

## Without the app

If you can't open the app, email [your support email] from the address linked to your account with the subject "Delete my account". We will delete your account and data within [30] days and confirm by email.

## What is deleted

- Your account (email and login)
- Your display name and profile
- Your posts, comments, and likes
- Your reports and blocks

Food preferences are saved only on your device. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data removes them.

## What may remain

Copies in system backups and server logs may remain briefly until they are overwritten in the normal course of operations. [Edit if this isn't accurate.]
